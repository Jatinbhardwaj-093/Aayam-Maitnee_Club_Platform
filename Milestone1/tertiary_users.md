# Tertiary Users Identification

Tertiary users interact with the system primarily for collaboration, sponsorship, outreach, and partnership activities. They are external stakeholders who are not involved in the club's internal management but engage with the club for mutual benefits.

### 1. Advertiser/ Sponser

Organizations, businesses, or individuals interested in promoting their products, services, or events through the drama club.

**Responsibilities include:**

* Viewing sponsorship opportunities offered by the club.
* Submitting sponsorship or advertisement requests.
* Uploading promotional materials and branding requirements.
* Tracking the status of sponsorship proposals.
* Receiving updates regarding event schedules and promotional activities.

**The system must provide** a sponsorship request portal, proposal tracking, secure document sharing, communication tools, and notification support to facilitate smooth collaboration.

### 2. Other Clubs / Industries / Organizations

Student clubs, NGOs, industries, or organizations seeking collaboration with the drama club for performances, awareness campaigns, cultural programs, or promotional events.

**Responsibilities include:**

* Browsing the club's portfolio and previous performances.
* Submitting collaboration requests.
* Proposing joint events or promotional campaigns.
* Communicating event requirements and schedules.
* Tracking the approval status of collaboration requests. 

**The system must provide** an online collaboration request system, event proposal management, messaging support, and status tracking for partnership activities.

### 3. Educational Institutions (Schools / Colleges)

Schools, colleges, or universities inviting the drama club to conduct theatre workshops, training sessions, or performances.

**Responsibilities include:**

* Viewing workshop offerings and performance portfolios.
* Requesting workshops or cultural performances.
* Specifying preferred dates, venue, and participant details.
* Communicating logistical requirements.
* Receiving confirmation and scheduling updates. 

**The system must provide** an institutional booking system, workshop request forms, scheduling tools, document sharing, and automated notifications.

# SMART User Stories

## Epic 1: Sponsorship Management
### User Story 1.1: New Sponsorship proposals

* **As** an Advertiser/Sponsor,
* **I want** to submit sponsorship proposals online,
* **So that** I can collaborate with the club without relying on manual communication.
* > **SMART Validation:**
    * **Specific:** Focuses on submitting sponsorship proposals through the system.
    * **Measurable:** The proposal is successfully submitted and acknowledged within 30 seconds.
    * **Achievable:** Uses an online proposal submission form connected to the club database.
    * **Relevant:** Eliminates manual emails and simplifies sponsorship management.
    * **Time-bound:** Used whenever sponsors wish to partner with the club before upcoming events.

### User Story 1.2: Uploding Promotional materials.

* **As  an Advertiser/Sponsor,**
* **I want** to upload promotional materials,
* **So that** the club can review and use them for approved events.
* > **SMART Validation:**
    * **Specific:** Focuses on uploading promotional content digitally.
    * **Measurable:** Files are uploaded successfully and become accessible to club administrators within 60 seconds.
    * **Achievable:** Uses standard document and media upload functionality.
    * **Relevant:** Prevents loss of promotional materials and centralizes sponsor resources.
    * **Time-bound:** Completed before sponsorship approval or event preparation.

### User Story 1.3: Status Tracking

* **As an Advertiser/Sponsor,**
* **I want to** track the status of my sponsorship proposal,
* **So that** I know whether it has been accepted, rejected, or requires modifications.
* > **SMART Validation:**
    * **Specific:** Focuses on viewing proposal status online.
    * **Measurable:** Status changes are reflected in the dashboard within 1 minute of an administrator's decision.
    * **Achievable:** Uses automatic status updates from the database.
    * **Relevant:** Improves transparency and reduces unnecessary follow-up emails.
    * **Time-bound:** Available throughout the sponsorship review process.

## Epic 3: Workshop & Performance Booking
### Story 3.1: Workshops

* **As an Educational Institution,**
* **I want to** browse available workshops and performances,
* **So that** I can select suitable programs for my students.
* > **SMART Validation:**
    * **Specific:** Focuses on viewing available workshops and performances.
    * **Measurable:** Workshop information loads within 5 seconds.
    * **Achievable:** Uses a searchable catalogue maintained by the club.
    * **Relevant:** Helps institutions make informed booking decisions.
    * **Time-bound:** Available throughout the academic year.

### Story 3.2: Booking Information

* **As a Educational Institution,** 
* **I want to** specify preferred dates, venues, and participant information,
* **So that** scheduling can be coordinated effectively.
* > **SMART Validation:**
    * **Specific:** Focuses on submitting booking requests digitally.
    * **Measurable:** Booking requests are recorded instantly and acknowledged within 30 seconds.
    * **Achievable:** Uses an online booking request form.
    * **Relevant:** Eliminates manual booking through phone calls and emails.
    * **Time-bound:** Used whenever institutions schedule workshops or performances.

### User Story 3.3: Booking Confirmations

* **As an Educational Institution,**
* **I want to** receive booking confirmations and schedule updates,
* **So** that I can plan the event accordingly.
* > **SMART Validation:**
    * **Specific:** Focuses on collecting scheduling details during booking.
    * **Measurable:** Submitted scheduling information is immediately available to club coordinators.
    * **Achievable:** Uses structured booking forms with required fields.
    * **Relevant:** Improves scheduling accuracy and reduces coordination delays.
    * **Time-bound:** Completed during the booking request process.
