# Cultural Club 

### Member Onboarding & Directory Management

1. How do new members join, register, and pay their fees? Where do they usually get stuck?
2. How do you update member records when details change, or when memberships expire?
3. How can members find and connect with others who share specific interests (like dance, music, or theatre)?

### Event Planning & Coordination

4. What was the biggest headache or challenge in planning your last major event?
5. How do you assign and coordinate duties (like setup, catering, or ticketing) among volunteers?

### Inventory, Equipment, & Space Booking

6. How do members book rehearsal spaces or borrow club equipment (instruments, costumes, etc.)?
7. Have you had booking conflicts where two groups tried to use the same space or gear at once? How did it happen?
8. What happens when club items or gear get lost or damaged? How do you track who is responsible?

### Communication & Member Engagement

9. How do you send official announcements and updates without overloading members with WhatsApp notifications?
10. How do members discover local cultural events? Can they share neighborhood events with the club?
11. How do members share photos and videos from past events? What is frustrating about the current method?

### Finances, Sponsorships, & Governance

12. Does the club host its own events? Are they free or paid? If paid, how do you manage sales or tickets?
13. How does the club vote on decisions, like electing new leaders or changing rules?
14. How do you track expenses, receipts, and sponsorships at the end of the year? How much manual effort does it take?
