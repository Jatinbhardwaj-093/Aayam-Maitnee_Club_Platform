# 3. Tertiary Users

These users interact with the system primarily for collaboration, sponsorship, outreach, and partnership activities. They are external stakeholders who are not involved in the club's internal management but engage with the club for mutual benefits.

### Advertiser/ Sponser

Organizations, businesses, or individuals interested in promoting their products, services, or events through the drama club.

**Responsibilities include:**

* Viewing sponsorship opportunities offered by the club.
* Submitting sponsorship or advertisement requests.
* Uploading promotional materials and branding requirements.
* Tracking the status of sponsorship proposals.
* Receiving updates regarding event schedules and promotional activities.

**The system must provide** a sponsorship request portal, proposal tracking, secure document sharing, communication tools, and notification support to facilitate smooth collaboration.
---

### Other Clubs / Industries / Organizations

Student clubs, NGOs, industries, or organizations seeking collaboration with the drama club for performances, awareness campaigns, cultural programs, or promotional events.

**Responsibilities include:**

* Browsing the club's portfolio and previous performances.
* Submitting collaboration requests.
* Proposing joint events or promotional campaigns.
* Communicating event requirements and schedules.
* Tracking the approval status of collaboration requests. 

**The system must provide** an online collaboration request system, event proposal management, messaging support, and status tracking for partnership activities.

---

### Educational Institutions (Schools / Colleges)

Schools, colleges, or universities inviting the drama club to conduct theatre workshops, training sessions, or performances.

**Responsibilities include:**

* Viewing workshop offerings and performance portfolios.
* Requesting workshops or cultural performances.
* Specifying preferred dates, venue, and participant details.
* Communicating logistical requirements.
* Receiving confirmation and scheduling updates. 

**The system must provide** an institutional booking system, workshop request forms, scheduling tools, document sharing, and automated notifications.

# USER RESEARCH & PAIN POINT ANALYSIS

*(Based on interviews with Club Secretary, External Club Coordinators, Industry Representatives, and College Cultural Coordinators.)*

## 1. Research Objective

To understand the challenges faced by external stakeholders when collaborating with the drama club for sponsorships, workshops, performances, and promotional activities.

---

## 2. Key Observational Insights

### Observation 1: Collaboration Requests are Manual

Most collaboration requests are handled through emails or personal contacts, making it difficult to track proposals and responses.

### Observation 2: Limited Visibility of Club Activities

External organizations often lack access to updated information regarding the club's events, achievements, availability, and services.

### Observation 3: Communication Delays

Discussions regarding sponsorships, workshop schedules, and promotional campaigns frequently involve multiple communication channels, leading to delays and misunderstandings.

---

# 3. Stakeholder Pain Points

## 3.1 Advertiser / Sponsor

* No centralized platform to submit sponsorship proposals.
* Difficulty tracking proposal approval status.
* Promotional material is shared through multiple channels.
* Limited information about upcoming events suitable for sponsorship.
* Communication regarding sponsorships are slow.

**Core Issue:** Sponsorship management is fragmented.

---

## 3.2 Other Clubs / Industries / Organizations

* No standard process for requesting collaborations.
* Event proposals are frequently exchanged through emails.
* Collaboration status is difficult to track.
* Event planning discussions become scattered across multiple platforms.

**Core Issue:** Collaboration management is inefficient due to the absence of a centralized request and communication system.

---

# 4. Educational Institutions

* Limited visibility into available workshops and performances.
* Booking requests are managed manually.
* Difficulty coordinating suitable dates.
* Lack of structured process for sharing workshop requirements.

**Core Issue:** Workshop and performance booking processes are time-consuming and lack proper scheduling support.

---

# 5. Root Causes Identified

* No centralized collaboration platform
* Manual sponsorship and workshop request process
* Lack of automated notifications
* Fragmented communication channels
* No proposal status tracking
* Limited public information about club activities and services

---

# 6. Overall Conclusion

The drama club functions successfully but external stakeholders experience unnecessary delays and communication challenges when collaborating with the club. A centralized digital platform would streamline sponsorship management, collaboration requests, workshop bookings, and external communication while improving transparency, responsiveness, and long-term partnerships.
